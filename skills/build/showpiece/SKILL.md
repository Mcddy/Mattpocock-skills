# Showpiece

Make the subject govern the words, composition, assets and behaviour. Distinctiveness should make the work useful or expressive; a palette change and a layer of effects do not supply an organising idea. Preserve conventional controls and familiar structures when they help people act. A polished surface must still deliver the complete authorised workflow.

## Choose the right job

| Request | Lead method |
|---|---|
| Ambitious new page, portfolio, interactive showcase or substantial redesign with an open direction | Showpiece |
| Focused audit, screenshot critique, visual repair or polish of an existing interface | Anti-vibecoded UI, when available |
| Build the underlying app, API, shared data or working integration | Forge; use Showpiece for a separately scoped expressive surface |
| Numbered interface-code rules or a command-line interface | Interface Architect |
| Choose among rendered visual/wording options, without building the underlying product | Sample Picker |
| Capture an existing page in its declared states and widths | State Gallery |

If another skill is unavailable, name that boundary and do the authorised work with the methods available. Do not pretend routing was activated. An explicit request to elevate an existing tool can belong here, but its task, data and accepted direction survive. Routine settings or administration do not require a showcase signature.

## Production precedence and truth

Apply confirmed requirements, accessibility and delivery constraints, then accepted brand/direction and the task's real content/device, then useful reference mechanisms, then stylistic options. State a consequential conflict before recommending a design; do not demote a requirement because a convenient architecture or effect cannot meet it. In the brief, distinguish confirmed facts with their source, inferred choices with a reason, and preferences. Keep uncertainty visible where it affects a decision.

The expressive grammar and numeric examples in the references are optional recipes for suitable showcase work. They do not require an oversized hero, tiny labels, monochrome palette, twelve-column grid, illustration, WebGL, asymmetry, ambient motion or decorative signature in every product. A dense table, conventional form, quiet reading page or dominant canvas may be right. No font, border, gradient, texture or purposeful empty area is a defect by itself. Do not carry one project's treatment across unrelated products unless a shared identity is actually scoped.

- Preserve working routes, integration identifiers, data semantics, controls and accepted brand. Report a necessary behaviour change and verify its consequences.
- Never invent clients, awards, logos, reviews, qualifications, availability, metrics, operating history or capabilities. Fictional personas and sample material are allowed when identified where a reader could mistake them for real work. Use no unsolicited process/AI badges and make no false human-authorship claim.
- Interface labels and summaries claim no more than their source: sample, synthetic, candidate, measured and validated are different evidence levels. An exact quote proves its text, not the truth of its claim. A deterministic score does not validate demand.
- Mock, sample or cached provider output retains origin in metadata or a sidecar, the handoff, and the surface where someone would otherwise assume it is live. A produced file or toast does not prove the service succeeded.
- Honest illustrative wear, textures and hand marks are permitted. Never use them as proof of unobserved handmade provenance, real history or authorship.
- Camera, microphone and motion sensors need informed opt-in, an explanation before permission, local processing, no recording/upload, cleanup on exit, and denied/unsupported alternatives. Do not add a sensor merely to demonstrate a recipe.
- Use authorised tools and existing project conventions. This skill grants no publishing, spending, external contact, private-data transfer, account change or unexpected installation authority. Honour tool refusals and host browser restrictions.

## 1. Find the material and task

Write the audience, main decision/action, intended device and delivery surface, constraints, accepted direction and what is real versus sample. Inventory supplied facts/assets; observations; checkable domain facts; friction; and missing material. Identify the inconvenient fact an expert would expect rather than smoothing it away. Use [concept development](references/concept.md) when the direction is open or the material thin, and [editorial integration](references/editorial.md) for a portfolio or substantial project story.

Inspect the actual assets at thumbnail, intended display size and close range. For an imagery-led hero, test the strongest assets at the size the composition needs; later substitute the weakest real asset. If the idea collapses, change the idea, crop or presentation rather than relying on demo-grade imagery. Thin material can support exact typography, site plans, real data, a procedural form derived from a subject rule or the product itself. Never fabricate photography, people or results to fill it.

A missing fact can be omitted, requested when consequential, or shown as a gap at its decision. Continue useful independent work; do not pad the page around the absence. For an existing interface, first record routes, controls, state, validation, data and integrations, then preserve a recoverable before state for comparison.

## 2. Choose the organising idea

For substantial open-direction work, compare three ideas from the object, the hardest decision and the context of use. Each needs one sentence, what leads, what changes in layout/copy/behaviour, what happens on intent, how it recomposes on a narrow screen and what it gives up. A palette, mood or three variants of one structure count as one idea. Skip this exercise for narrow edits or an accepted direction.

Choose the idea that uses the strongest particulars, eases the real judgement, is honest about the material and can be built within scope with an accessible quiet alternative. Use the noun swap to expose interchangeable framing, without rejecting a useful conventional table or form. Write the idea and the five-second line a stranger should understand. Page architecture follows the question: sequence, comparison, index, reading surface or canvas as appropriate.

Study a few relevant reference states by task/work surface, density, device and interaction. Record the exact source/state, transferable mechanism and departure. A static capture shows visible composition; an exercised claim requires a named observed action/result or receipt. Do not infer motion, accessibility or live data from screenshots. Do not copy third-party composition, artwork, wordmarks, text or signature effects one to one.

For a genuine showcase, a memorable signature can focus the idea: an object, spatial image composition, container, scrubbed sequence, participation, typography or saturated field. Combine only what serves the same idea and keep one leading. A signature is not mandatory for utility work. Read [showcase patterns](references/showcase-patterns.md) only when expressive direction or system choices need those catalogues. Preserve accepted direction unless the user reopens it or rejects the result.

## 3. Derive the system and write the copy

Use one or two governing rules from the subject: a useful proportion, unit, content length, actual date axis or action duration. The rule must reveal a relationship, not merely explain a hidden rationale. Establish reading order, comparison density, navigation, primary action and relevant subject imagery before decoration. Keep the first meaningful task inspectable at its intended viewport; do not let chrome or spectacle crowd the deciding information.

Reuse coherent project tokens. Define compact semantic roles for type, colour, spacing, shape, states and motion. Test the actual words, long values, narrow layouts and fallbacks. Use display type where it earns attention and legible operational text elsewhere. Zoom must enlarge text. Intentional typographic/image bleed must not cut off a needed label, amount or control, create sideways page scroll, or hide content from assistive technology. Detailed fitting, grid, typography and motion examples live in [showcase patterns](references/showcase-patterns.md); they are choices rather than global specifications.

Draft real strings before layout. Read [craft and behaviour](references/craft.md) for substantial composition/assets/interaction. Headings orient; actions predict their consequence; statuses name the instance; errors explain recovery. Distinguish typed input, observations, computations and suggestions. Give each deciding fact one primary home beside its action, repeating only for an independent context such as review, receipt or recovery. Keep useful domain language and conventional Save/Cancel labels.

Preserve quantities, uncertainty, distinct fees/states, reasoning, supplied voice, quotations, code, identifiers, links and required notices. Fix the layout rather than cutting a qualifier. Replace stock praise with its supported consequence or remove it. Alternatives must differ by what changes the choice; recommendations need reasons and real trade-offs. Keep internal build/QA vocabulary out of the product unless its users need it. Collect the relevant states as one string sheet and check source, delta, transplant, swap, argument and voice; do not flatten the voice into clipped fragments.

## 4. Build a complete experience

Prove one demanding slice first: real or labelled content, the primary action, a relevant hard state and the result, rendered and exercised at target sizes where possible. For a one-screen job this can be the deliverable; otherwise it is a checkpoint, not permission to omit the rest of the authorised workflow.

Model input, state, representation, computed output and consequence before effects. Cover applicable default, selected, hover, focus, pressed, disabled, busy, loading, empty, no-results, partial, invalid, error, refused, success, unavailable and recovery states; include interruption, dismissal and focus return. Selection, visible artwork, labels, price, summary and export must describe the same state. Inspect the actual picture and computed visibility; a changed accessible name alone can conceal the wrong artwork.

Every action produces its promised consequence or honestly explains unavailability. Verify persistence, provider success and exported output semantically, including reload/restart where relevant. Keep unknown outcomes unknown until re-read. Preserve valid input and provide a useful recovery path on refusal, denied storage or unsupported capability. Do not replace a live integration with a plausible-looking sample and call the workflow complete.

Recompose around the next decision at narrow widths. Keep controls and their results related without a fixed band covering content. Reserve space so controls do not move under a pointer or finger. Cheap computations may update directly as input changes; invalid input retains a clearly identified last valid result or an explicit invalid state. A button remains appropriate when computation is expensive, consequential or explicitly requested.

Use native controls/semantics, labels, visible unobscured focus, appropriate activation keys, keyboard alternatives to drag/hover, dismissal/focus return and state announcements. Motion or scrolling cannot gate essential information. Text/table equivalents preserve the meaning of a visual object. Keep document reading, keyboard paging, anchors, find-in-page and zoom usable.

## 5. Add expressive mechanics with resilient fallbacks

Prefer existing approved project tooling. Before adding a library or effect, check capability, delivery support, loading cost and the flow it serves; verify current APIs/licensing against official documentation for the chosen version. Pin compatible dependencies when needed. Technical recipes and historical version notes are in [implementation patterns](references/implementation-patterns.md), including fitted type, WebGL objects, all fifteen expressive recipes, stress testing and early-frame capture. They are not authority to install software, change security flags or bypass host tools.

Real assets lead. Created diagrams/imagery use a consistent vocabulary tied to the subject and are inspected at final size and thumbnail. Check cut-out edges, proportions, crops, text equivalents and generated-image disclosures where they affect truth. Image sequences need a poster and staged loading; models need suitable compression, lighting, content framing and disposal. Unsupported WebGL/camera/network paths keep a useful static or pointer alternative and truthful origin.

Each motion has a trigger, relationship, treatment/timing, interruption behaviour, final state and reduced-motion alternative. Read [motion contract](references/motion.md) for substantial movement. Repeated actions stay quick and quiet; cancellation or a stale callback must not restore old state. Content exists before its entrance. Pause autonomous movement lasting over five seconds with a visible usable control; pause work offscreen/in hidden tabs. Reduced motion preserves feedback and meaning without large movement or unnecessary loops.

Keep the native cursor for text/controls, avoid previews obscuring content, and retain a non-gesture route to every item. A showcase scrub or optional smooth-scroll enhancement must preserve native operation, anchors and reading; never change scroll distance or make the core task depend on a long effect. Intro/poster loading cannot block contact, price, reading or the primary action. Render on demand where possible, bound canvas resolution, release resources on exit and handle context loss.

## 6. Verify, judge and revise

Read [verification and revision](references/verification.md) and [acceptance](references/acceptance.md). Use [independent review methods](references/review.md) when comparison, research-informed evaluation or an explicitly requested origin screen is consequential. Consult [evidence](references/evidence.md) only for research-dependent claims; historical findings do not establish this product's outcomes or validate this skill.

Choose proportionate checks on authorised surfaces. For interactive work, exercise the main journey, hard state, refusal/failure and recovery; keyboard order/activation/focus return; touch, contrast, long content, 320 CSS-pixel reflow where applicable, actual text enlargement/200% zoom and reduced motion. Test repeated inputs, boundaries, cancellation, resize and theme/view changes at real speed. Check stale output, detached labels, jumping controls and console errors. Verify the no-WebGL/no-JS or equivalent promised fallback and first usable paint on a constrained connection when relevant.

Use approved rendering tools at intended desktop/mobile viewports and inspect first viewport plus relevant complete states. A contact sheet is not actual phone-scale or real-device evidence. Software/headless graphics can establish captured composition/state, not real-device smoothness. Name the environment for performance measurements; small files, transform usage, animation-frame intervals or a screenshot do not establish field INP, GPU frame delivery, Core Web Vitals or 60fps. If rendering is unavailable, finish usable sources and state exactly what remains untested.

For each check retain tool/command/environment, intended semantic observation, actual result and coverage. Distinguish completed, partial, not-applicable, failed and runner-error; retain the native spelling/exit code and any usage error explicitly. A missing dependency or incomplete scan is never a product pass. Hashes prove custody, not that the intended item or action was selected. Selected accessibility checks are not certification.

After functional checks, judge cold read, task hierarchy, subject specificity, material quality, intentional scale/density, restraint, expert/buyer information, fact placement, copy, close-range craft and feel. For an expressive brief also ask what memorable event carries it and whether effects compete. Substitute the weakest real asset; strip effects to check structure; inspect the close/footer as carefully as the hero. Verify every criticism against the actual artefact and record location, observation, consequence, correction and check. A measured defect, design assessment and preference are different findings; no authorship score substitutes for quality.

Fix the largest supported weakness, then repeat affected checks. Revisit the organising idea only for an open-direction brief; accepted defect-only work keeps its direction. If the owner still rejects a repaired open-direction candidate, compare another idea with the same content/sizes rather than recolouring. For a claim of improvement, freeze the same brief, constraints, tools, revision budget and defect categories before production/judging, hide version labels, count defects/corrections/time and retain disagreement. Report no improvement shown when warranted. Agent assessment is not human acceptance.

## Deliver

Provide editable sources and a runnable/openable result; the idea in one sentence; important decisions tied to purpose; accurately labelled before/after for existing work; and any behaviour changed. Inspect final exports at intended ratio, native platform crop and thumbnail, keeping wrappers from crowding the work. A representative populated/active state may lead a demo while purposeful first-use/empty/recovery states remain available. For suitable launch/showcase work, supply 9:16 and 16:9 captures when useful and cheap, with meaningful motion frames rather than arbitrary crops.

Keep source-inspected, rendered, exercised, measured, externally verified, agent-assessed and untested distinct. Captures match the actual build and claims. Record human review as Pass, Revise or Not exercised from a real response; silence is not approval. Origin screening runs only on explicit request, with frozen candidates, neutral independent conditions and literal results; retain accessibility/truth, never manufacture errors or stories to influence it. Detailed rules are in [review](references/review.md).

Keep receipts, engineering notes and unresolved verification gaps outside product flows except material limits at decisions. Do not promise awards, superior quality, user preference, a classification or commercial outcomes. The [illustrative adversarial cases](references/adversarial-cases.md) are acceptance proposals, not tests that have run.
