# Writing, composition and experience as one idea

Choose what the work contributes before making it sound polished. Write an editorial position: who needs it, their question, decisive observation, useful result and boundary of the claim. Preserve exact source material, factual qualifiers and supplied voice. Missing facts may be omitted, requested or uncertain; never filled with invented biography, commissions or outcomes.

Develop a specific argument or project story. An operational case, comparison and personal reflection can be different organising ideas; five promotional headlines around the same concept are not. Use supplied detail that changes interpretation rather than ornamental precision. Numbers earn space when they matter to a choice, not because they look authoritative.

Integrate language with the visual: headline orients; dominant image proves or explains; controls predict effects; errors offer repair; optional depth earns attention. An outer caption should not repeat the image's headline. Inspect line breaks, truncation, punctuation, adjacent cadence and repeated paragraph shapes. Keep technical vocabulary in products that need it; do not blindly replace evidence terminology in research tools.

Portfolio examples:

- Weak: “Useful digital work made reviewable. Evidence you can inspect.” This identifies the evaluator's process rather than the service.
- Candidate: “Small websites and browser tools”, followed by a concrete project and active interaction. Retain only supported service categories.
- Weak: a page of CSV fixture rows presented as a cleanup outcome.
- Candidate: before/after excerpt with changed fields, retained records and a separate exception report, in buyer terms. Exact source traces stay in supporting documentation. Do not call unresolved records import-ready.

No automatic AI-process badge or disclaimer. Keep relevant personal/fictional scope and functionality limits where they inform interpretation; repeat only when a new context needs them. Local drafts do not submit orders; sample prices are not real commercial offers. Keep mandatory notices and safety information.

For skeptical readers, ask what is interchangeable, unsupported, inflated or arbitrarily selected. Repair the work, not its origin story. Do not add awkwardness, typos, fake anecdotes or authorship claims. The result needs no explanatory performance about how hard it was to make.
