# Reference method: structure from a shipped product of the same kind

Step 4b of `SKILL.md`. The aim is a screen whose structure works the way good products of its kind work. It is not meant to look like any one of them.

## Choose by work surface

Start from the structure contract: the task, its main object, the device and the first meaningful action. Pick one primary reference whose work surface, platform, density and interaction match. Add at most two supporting references for a specific mechanism. Ignore colour and brand when choosing, because the look comes from the board, the assignment or the user's brand.

When the user has supplied a reference pack, choose from its manifest and note the exact image and its evidence type. Otherwise reason from the mechanisms below.

## Evidence types

| Type | What it shows | What it doesn't |
|---|---|---|
| **Exercised** | A named interaction and resulting state with an actual observation or receipt | Other actions, states or motion that were not exercised |
| **Static capture, interaction unverified** | The visible layout, content and density in that exact image | Whether controls work, data is live, or other states exist |
| **Official demo or illustration** | Intended layout | The live product |
| **Marketing still** | Composition and hierarchy | Working interaction; its marketing frame and text are not the interface |

## What transfers, and what never does

- **Transfers:** hierarchy (what leads); navigation (one way per axis); where the primary action sits; disclosure (what is hidden until needed); density (how much shows at once); feedback (where results appear).
- **Never:** logos, artwork, illustrations, photography, marketing frames, product names, copy, or a whole layout reproduced.

## Depart when the product differs

The analog informs the structure; it doesn't dictate it. Where this product's data, workflow, device or delivery differ, depart and say why in the structure contract. For an offline field app without available map data, grouping or a schematic may serve better than unavailable tiles; cached maps remain valid when the task and delivery support them. A tool for two people does not need a team product's sidebar.

## No universal recipe

There is no default list-and-dashboard, and no global ban on borders, cards, gradients or expressive type. A dense triage table may need thin rules. An editorial page may need expressive type and imagery. A canvas tool's large empty area can be correct. Judge by the task, not by a style rule.

## Mechanisms by work surface

These are in forge's own words. No third-party images are included: reference packs are the user's private study material.

| Work surface | Mechanisms that usually transfer |
|---|---|
| **Today list** (tasks, rounds, jobs) | The current or next item leads, with its main action beside it; the rest follows in order; quick capture is one step away; done items step back rather than vanish; the empty day is a calm, designed state |
| **Place and status** (jobs, trips, deliveries) | A list ordered by time or route, with place as grouping or a schematic when no map is available; one status card for the item in focus; status words and times beside the action |
| **Dense triage** (inbox, tickets, reviews) | One row per item at reading density; status and age in fixed columns; filters above, with counts; a detail pane beside the list, not a page change; keyboard operation throughout; thin rules where they separate rows |
| **Linked input and output** (calculators, simulators) | Inputs and output visible together; output updates as you type; selecting in one view highlights in the other; settings disclosed in place without losing the output; numbers stated, not only drawn |
| **Side-by-side comparison** | Two states aligned on the same axes; the difference stated as a number; one control to swap or adjust each side |
| **Canvas and tool rail** (editors) | The canvas dominates; a stable tool rail; options appear for the current selection only; the empty canvas invites the first action |
| **Editorial and media** | Content leads at large scale; type carries hierarchy; navigation recedes until it is needed |
