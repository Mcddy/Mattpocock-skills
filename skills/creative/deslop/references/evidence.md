# Evidence behind the craft checks

Prepared 17 October 2026. These sources inform conditional design choices and evaluation methods. They do not validate these skills, identify authorship or establish a universal visual style. The distinction between a study finding and an editorial application matters.

| Evidence | Practical use | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| [Sayfi et al., 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38008266/), randomised adult plain-language trial, N=488 | Check whether someone can explain the content or act correctly. | WHO-content understanding improved, while the CDC comparison was not significant. Wording and format were bundled. Preference is a different outcome. |
| [Sadoski, Goetz and Rodriguez, 2000](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-0663.92.1.85), concreteness across text types | Explain an abstraction with relevant examples, named operations and perceptible consequences. | Advantages differed across genres; selected texts varied in more than one feature. Invented detail supplies no evidence. |
| [Fazio et al., 2015](https://www.apa.org/pubs/journals/features/xge-0000098.pdf), repetition and truth judgements | Keep claims verifiable even when phrasing is fluent or repeated. | Laboratory statement ratings do not establish product behaviour. Repetition for context, labels, learning or distinct decisions can be useful. |
| [Dillard and Shen, 2005](https://doi.org/10.1080/03637750500111815), health-message reactance | Give reasons and genuine trade-offs; retain real choice. | Context-specific health-message studies do not diagnose an audience or prove a conversion effect. Clear requirements still need direct instructions. |
| [Ziefle, 2010](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20382372/), small-screen navigation, N=40 | Balance legibility with visible overview. | Large type worked differently with broad versus narrow menu preview. Historic device and selected sample do not define a modern font-size prescription. |
| [Grahame, Laberge and Scialfa, 2004](https://doi.org/10.1518/hfes.46.3.385.50404), targeted page search | Remove competing emphasis and irrelevant choices while retaining task context. | Small historic experiment; not a prohibition on density, graphics or multiple options. |
| [Xie et al., 2017](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0183884), multimedia cueing synthesis | Use cues to reveal a relationship; test comprehension and changed application separately from ease. | Outcomes differed and studies were heterogeneous. Pooled estimates are not promised product gains. |
| [Dekkers et al., 2021](https://www.jmir.org/2021/3/e15846/), information architecture, N=215 | Choose a structure for the task and compare credible alternatives. | Direct knowledge and satisfaction contrasts were not significant. Some modelled pathways differed. Neither result licenses a universal architecture rule. |
| [Darejeh et al., 2026](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13219774/), cognitive-load measurement review | Define task correctness, consequential errors and recovery before choosing supplementary measures. | Frequency of a measure is not its validity. Gaze, confidence, speed and low reported effort cannot alone certify understanding. |

## Actual local dataset reanalysis

Source: [SuperEnglish/SimpleEnglish Telemetry and Cognitive Load Dataset](https://zenodo.org/records/19926382), Jos Timanta Tarigan, 2026, CC BY 4.0 as stated by the custodian. [Public codebook](https://zenodo.org/records/19926382/files/README.md?download=1).

The authorised local reanalysis read the existing Telemetry sheet only, without demographic or questionnaire rows. The workbook's SHA-256 was unchanged. The analysis exports aggregate descriptions, without respondent identifiers.

The dataset contains 96 participants, 48 per application, and 632 repeated session records. SimpleEnglish supplied 315 sessions and SuperEnglish 317. Each person first contributed their mean across observed sessions, after which the 48 person means were described with equal weight.

The public codebook describes each row as a quiz session. The sheet itself has no timestamp or session identifier. Time summaries are means of per-row means within each person, not pooled raw answer latencies.

| Condition | Mean / median accuracy | Mean / median answer time, seconds |
|---|---:|---:|
| Plain SimpleEnglish | 0.7901 / 0.8000 | 5.7723 / 5.7595 |
| Gamified SuperEnglish | 0.8098 / 0.8146 | 5.7361 / 5.7592 |

These are descriptive observations. No significance, equivalence or treatment-effect claim was made. Adaptive task/difficulty exposure and numbers of sessions differ. Random assignment was not established from the public documentation. One short session with a narrow convenience sample does not measure retention or broad design preferences. Animation, sound and thematic visuals were bundled. Questionnaire reliability concerns and unresolved public documentation placeholders remain.

The methodological lesson is to compare relevant outcomes in the intended task. These data do not establish that minimalism, gamification or any one design feature is generally superior. Repeated sessions are not independent people. Detailed aggregate methods and receipts are retained in the accompanying local project, separately from these portable skill instructions.

## Editorial and design judgement

Rules about stock phrasing, transplanted headlines, arbitrary cards, broken text wraps and duplicated hierarchy are craft judgements. They are not neurological laws. A word such as signal, a familiar layout, a gradient, a long sentence or a repeated label is acceptable when it communicates something useful in context. Ask what it means and what job it does.

Agent review is different from a human usability study. Source inspection, rendering, exercised actions, measured outcomes and human judgement should be reported at their actual evidence level.
