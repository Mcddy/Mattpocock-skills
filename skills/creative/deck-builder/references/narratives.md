# Narrative structures

## Pitch or persuasion
1. Hook (the problem or opportunity)
2. Why now
3. The solution
4. How it works
5. Proof or evidence
6. Business model or value proposition
7. Traction or social proof
8. The ask

## Executive briefing
1. Situation
2. Complication (why it matters)
3. Key question
4. Analysis (3 to 5 findings)
5. Recommendation
6. Next steps

## Informational or educational
1. Title and agenda
2. Context: why this matters
3. Core content (3 to 5 themes)
4. Key takeaways
5. Questions and resources

## Investor deck
1. Company purpose in one sentence
2. Problem
3. Solution
4. Why now
5. Market size
6. Product
7. Business model
8. Traction
9. Team
10. The ask
