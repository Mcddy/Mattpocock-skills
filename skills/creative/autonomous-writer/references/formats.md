# Formats

## Long-form articles and reports
- Lead with the most important insight (inverted pyramid or a hook).
- Clear hierarchy of sections and subsections with logical flow.
- Evidence, examples and specifics, never vague assertions.
- End with a payoff: conclusion, call to action or open question.
- Short paragraphs, varied sentence length, active voice.

## Newsletter or Substack (Cerberus Intelligence)
- Match the three-lens voice: macro, technical, sentiment.
- Lead with the market question of the week.
- Build the analysis through each lens in turn.
- Close with a clear positioning stance or trade thesis, framed as analysis.
- Tone: authoritative, direct, institutional, not casual.

## Sales copy
- Lead with the problem, not the product; make the cost of the problem concrete before the solution.
- Specific proof (real numbers, outcomes, testimonials supplied by him), not vague claims.
- Answer objections directly.
- One call to action.
- Write several headlines or hooks and pick the best, offering the runner-up.

## Emails
- Subject line: write 3, pick the best.
- First sentence earns the second.
- One ask per email.
- Say it in half the words first thought of.

## Scripts (video, audio, podcast)
- Write for the ear: shorter sentences, natural rhythm, room to breathe.
- Hook in the first 15 seconds.
- Structure: hook, context, content, close.
- Delivery notes only where they matter.

## Proposals and pitches
- Open with the client's world, not credentials.
- State the problem precisely, then the solution.
- Evidence of capability.
- Clear ask and obvious next step.
