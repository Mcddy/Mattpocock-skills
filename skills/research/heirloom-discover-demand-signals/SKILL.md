# Discover Demand Signals

Require the candidate user/workflow, accessible observation records and the decision being made. If no real observations exist, create clearly synthetic workflow examples for learning and mark demand unmeasured. Local geography or an ABN does not prove customer access, willingness to pay or a market size.

## Separate signals from stories

1. Inventory observations with origin, date, user role, triggering task, workaround, frequency, consequence and evidence location. Separate direct observation, participant report, public complaint and your inference. Preserve who can authorise access to the relevant process/data.
2. Group by problem mechanism, not product feature. Repeated manual copying, exception handling and missing status are different jobs even if all suggest a dashboard. Deduplicate syndicated posts or one participant's repeated comment so volume is not mistaken for independent demand.
3. Distinguish frustration, repeated workaround, active search, budget/authority and actual commitment. Do not translate likes, compliments or traffic into revenue. For a public claim, retain a source/date; current competitive context needs browsing if it matters.
4. Identify the smallest improvement observable in an accessible workflow: fewer re-entry steps, shorter exception resolution or less lost evidence. Specify what a solo-use synthetic prototype can prove and what requires an authorised participant.
5. Rank at most a few hypotheses by evidence access, recurrence, consequence, existing workaround and maintenance burden. Use ordinal assessments with reasons. Missing buying evidence remains GAP; avoid numerical market estimates from tiny samples.
6. Design one discriminating next observation within the time ceiling the task actually has (60 to 120 minutes is a default for when none is given). Give pass/fail/inconclusive updates. Draft questions or a demo locally; this skill does not authorise contacting people, scraping private systems or sending outreach.

## Deliver

Write `demand-signal-ledger.csv` and `problem-hypotheses.md`. Rows include signal_id, evidence_kind, source_ref, role, workflow, recurrence_basis, consequence, independence_family and confidence. Each hypothesis names excluded claims, access gap, proposed proof and kill condition. Preserve rejected hypotheses and why they lost.

## Acceptance and worked situation

Require one observed recurring workaround that supports investigation and a negative control containing enthusiastic feedback without a concrete workflow or commitment. Do not label either as a sale. Check independence families and distinguish proof the prototype runs from proof the user values it. Passing supports a next investigation, not commercial viability.

Situation: three posts repeat one complaint about copying booking details. Treat them as one public signal. A permitted observation of weekly re-entry is stronger workflow evidence, but buying authority is still unknown. Build a small local reconciliation demo before suggesting a business launch.
