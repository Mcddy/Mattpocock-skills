# Network mapping and signals

For weighing competing explanations of what the signals mean, use intelligence-analyst. Use public or freely given information only.

## Network mapping

Nodes:
- Tier 1 core (central figures, formal leaders).
- Tier 2 secondary (supporting players with access or capability).
- Tier 3 peripheral.

Edges: type (authority, influence, information, resource, trust, historical, adversarial), strength (strong, moderate, weak, unknown), direction (A to B, B to A, both).

Centrality:
- Degree: most connections; broad reach, not always deep influence.
- Betweenness: sits between clusters; brokers who control information flow. Highest strategic value.
- Closeness: reaches everyone fastest; spreads information.
- Influence: whose opinion changes decisions; often a quiet node.

Vulnerabilities and leverage points: single points of failure (removal fragments the network), bridge nodes, weak edges, a broker who gives access to several clusters, a trusted adviser to a decision-maker, the person everyone confides in.

Entry path: the fewest honest steps from Shaun's current position to the objective node (introduction, shared project, public contribution). Mark the confidence on each edge and how it was learned.

## Signals

- Said: what is said, and what is conspicuously not said (an expected denial that is absent).
- Digital behaviour in public: posting frequency, response times, platform changes, who they connect with.
- Organisational behaviour: hiring patterns, partnerships, product direction, departures.

Traffic patterns: volume (a sudden rise suggests urgency or a new initiative, a fall suggests silence or disruption); timing (late-night activity suggests pressure, a shifted pattern suggests changed circumstances); direction (new channels mean new relationships, quiet old ones mean cooling); frequency of a topic (growing concern or opportunity).

Picture template:

```
SIGNALS: each with date and source
PATTERNS: what emerges across several
HYPOTHESES: H1 and at least one alternative that fits equally
SIGNAL TO HYPOTHESIS: which signals support which
ASSESSMENT: most likely picture, confidence, signals to watch next
```
