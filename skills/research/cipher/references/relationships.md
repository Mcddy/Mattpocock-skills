# Relationships

## Develop

**Spot.** Who is worth developing?
- Access: what knowledge, opportunity or influence do they hold?
- Reachability: can Shaun reach them from his current position?
- Value trajectory: will the relationship compound?
- Tiers: 1 direct critical access, 2 indirect high value, 3 ambient network.

**Assess.** Understand before approaching. Motivations (MICE): Money or resources (career or income focus); Ideology or mission (values-led); Coercion (a pressure on them to relieve, never exploit); Ego or recognition (status, being seen). Most professional relationships are mission plus recognition.

**Develop.** First contact is low commitment and high value to them: give before taking. Rapport is genuine, not tactical: remember specifics and be consistent. Each interaction is slightly more invested than the last. Asks come when the relationship can bear them and feel natural, not transactional.

**Maintain.** See below.

## States

Development (building trust), Active (mutual trust, delivering value), Dormant (intact, inactive, with a reactivation route), Ended (lessons noted).

## Cadence

| Tier | Frequency | Maximum gap |
|---|---|---|
| 1 inner circle | weekly | 3 weeks |
| 2 high-value professional | monthly | 6 weeks |
| 3 network | quarterly | annually |

## Health

- Healthy: they initiate, share voluntarily, make introductions, advocate in your absence. Maintain.
- Stressed: slower and shorter replies, declined meetings, less sharing. Raise frequency and the value you bring.
- Troubled: no initiation, formal contact only, closer to a competitor. Have a direct conversation or reclassify.

Audit motivation quarterly: is the original motive still live, what do they value now, what would renew their side?
