# Workshop feedback: findings

Response analysis: counts over named denominators, with intervals.

SYNTHETIC data. Written by the authoring session from `analysis.txt`, `follow-up-by-mode.txt` and `themes.txt` in this folder. Not independently checked.

## Answer

The workshop worked for most of the people who answered, and attending online was a clearly worse experience. Fix the online setup and slow the hands-on exercise before running the follow-up.

- FACT. 18 of the 22 who answered rated it Useful or Very useful (82%, 95% interval 61% to 93%). 1 of 23 skipped the question.
- FACT. 7 of 9 online respondents found the pace a bit or much too fast, against 3 of 14 in person. The difference is 56 points, 95% interval 15 to 77.
- FACT. 5 of the 8 online respondents who answered had audio or connection problems. 1 of the 9 skipped it.
- ASSESSMENT, moderate confidence. The pace complaint is mostly an online problem, not a content problem. It rests on 9 online respondents, and the low end of that interval is 15 points.

## Who answered

23 of the 38 attendees (61%), through an anonymous emailed form with one reminder. GAP: nothing is known about the other 15. People who left early, or had no strong view, are probably under-represented, so these figures describe the 23 who replied and are not "82% of attendees".

14 attended in person and 9 online. 18 of 23 answered every closed question shown to them, and no closed question lost more than 2 respondents. The audio question was shown only to the 9 online respondents; the 14 in person were routed past it and are not non-response.

Cases that do not fit. R15 asked whether there is a certificate, which is a question and carries no code. R20 is the one respondent who rated the session Not useful, and their comments say it was pitched at beginners. One person is not a pattern, but it is the only clear statement that the level was wrong, and it argues for saying in the invitation who the session is for.

Privacy check. R20's comments gave their role and an email address. Both were removed in the coded sheet and neither is quoted here. No other passage names a person, a workplace or a role.

## What these responses cannot show

- GAP. Whether the 15 who did not reply felt the same.
- GAP. Whether online attendees learned less. Confidence and exercise ratings were not split by mode; with 9 online respondents the pace split is the only comparison examined, and reporting several splits to find one that separates would mislead.
- GAP. Whether pace or audio caused the lower ratings. The form asked both at once, of the same people.

## Next

1. Before the follow-up: a room microphone, a camera on the whiteboard, and one person watching the online questions.
2. Slow the exercise, or add a checkpoint after step three.
3. Send the files the day before.
4. Ask the same pace and audio questions next time. The online group is small enough that one more session will sharpen the difference or dissolve it.
