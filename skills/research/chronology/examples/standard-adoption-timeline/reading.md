**Chronology, dates as precise as their sources.** EN 301 549 and EU accessibility law: 30 events from 13 sources, 2014 to 2026. 5 date conflict pairs, 1 of them open. 1 event undated. Sources read on 9 October 2026.

The question was how the standard has lined up in time with the law it supports.

**Under the 2016 Directive the order was W3C text, then standard, then citation, twice.** ASSESSMENT, high confidence in the order: each step is weeks or months apart, so month precision does not blur it.

- WCAG 2.1 on 5 Jun 2018 (E05), EN 301 549 V2.1.2 in Aug 2018 (E06), referenced in the Official Journal on 21 Dec 2018 (E07). Publication to citation: between 112 and 142 days, the range that "Aug 2018" allows.
- V3.2.1 in Mar 2021 (E14), the amending decision adopted 11 Aug 2021 (E17) and published 12 Aug 2021 (E18). Publication to citation: between 134 and 164 days.

**Under the 2019 Act the order is reversed, and the dates contradict any account in which a matching standard was ready when the Act began to apply.** The Act's measures applied from 28 Jun 2025 (E24, Article 31(2)). V4.1.1 was adopted on 24 Aug 2026 (E26) and published on 2 Sep 2026 (E28): 431 days after the Act applied. On 18 Sep 2026 a law firm note said it was not yet cited in the Official Journal (E29, REPORTED). The checker reports "E28 before E24" as VIOLATED.

**Two orderings cannot be decided at the recorded precision.**

- V3.2.1 adopted (E13, 10 Mar 2021) against published (E14, Mar 2021). Adoption would be expected first, but the cover gives only the month, so the dates do not show it.
- Standardisation request M/587 (E20, 2022) against the transposition deadline (E21, 28 Jun 2022). The only source for the request gives the year.

**Three gaps longer than 365 days.** ASSESSMENT: each is a gap in what was read, not a finding that nothing happened.

- At least 545 days from 30 Apr 2015 to 26 Oct 2016, and at least 550 days from 2 Dec 2016 to 5 Jun 2018: the Directive's passage, its transposition, and the drafting of V2.1.2 under Mandate 554. No dated source for the mandate was read.
- At least 434 days from 5 Oct 2023 to 12 Dec 2024: nothing read is dated in this stretch. Separately, ETSI's directory lists folders for a version 4.1.0 stamped in November 2025 and June 2026. What those stamps mark was not verified, so they are not recorded as events.

**Open date conflict.** When V3.2.1 became the harmonised standard: 12 Aug 2021 by the EUR-Lex record of the decision (E18, primary), 18 Aug 2021 by the running text of the Commission's policy page, twice (E19, secondary). The same page's table says 12th August. ASSESSMENT, moderate confidence: 12 August is right and the page text is a slip. Nothing read explains 18 August; the Official Journal issue itself would settle it.

**Explained pairs.** E07 and E08: the policy page dates the 2018 reference to 20 Dec, the date in the decision's title, where EUR-Lex records publication on 21 Dec (classed announcement against occurrence, which is an inference). E15 and E16, E24 and E25, E27 and E28: one source gives the day and another the month, or a relative date; the dates are compatible.

**Undated:** when V3.1.1 was submitted for harmonisation (U01). The policy page says it was, and that it was never referenced.

**Not established:** the Commission's own record of M/587 (its page returned metadata only); the annex of the 2018 decision naming V2.1.2 (not returned; the version is taken from the policy page); whether V4.1.1 has been cited since 18 Sep 2026.

The two earlier citations each came four to five and a half months after publication. Whether that carries over to V4.1.1 is a forecasting question, not something this timeline answers.
