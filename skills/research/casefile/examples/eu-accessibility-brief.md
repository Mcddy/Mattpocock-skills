### Q1: Which standard is harmonised for websites and mobile applications under the Web Accessibility Directive?

- [FACT] EN 301 549 V3.2.1 (2021-03) is the harmonised standard for websites and mobile applications under Directive (EU) 2016/2102, the Web Accessibility Directive. (C1; S1; Title of the decision; annex entry for EN 301 549 V3.2.1 (2021-03)) Load-bearing.

### Q3: What does WCAG 2.2 success criterion 2.5.8 require?

- [FACT] WCAG 2.2 SC 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum) is Level AA and has five exceptions: spacing, equivalent, inline, user agent control and essential. (C4; S2; Success criterion text at the top of the page) Load-bearing.

### Q4: How should the target-size criterion be described in guidance?

- [ASSESSMENT, moderate confidence] Do not describe SC 2.5.8, or any pixel size, as an EU legal requirement. Describe it as the WCAG 2.2 Level AA criterion. (C6) Load-bearing.
