HL-101 Mounting heights

Edition 3, in force from 1 March 2026.

1 Scope

This standard sets the mounting height of fixed navigation lights on harbour walls up to 8 metres high.

2 Requirement

A fixed light shall be mounted at least 4.0 metres above mean high water.

Where the wall is lower than 4.0 metres, the light shall be mounted on a mast.
