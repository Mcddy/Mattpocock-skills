Published standards

The Board publishes four standards for navigation lighting in small harbours.

  • HL-101 Mounting heights
  • HL-102 Lamp colour
  • HL-103 Inspection intervals
  • HL-104 Backup power

HL-100 was withdrawn in 2025 and replaced by HL-101 and HL-102.
