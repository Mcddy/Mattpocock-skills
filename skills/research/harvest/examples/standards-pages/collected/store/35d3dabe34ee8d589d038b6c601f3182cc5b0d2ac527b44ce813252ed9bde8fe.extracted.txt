Home Standards Committee

HL-103 Inspection intervals

Edition 2, in force from 1 October 2026.

2 Requirement

Each fixed light shall be inspected at least once every 6 months.

A record of each inspection shall be kept for 5 years.

Edition 2 replaces the 12 month interval of edition 1.
