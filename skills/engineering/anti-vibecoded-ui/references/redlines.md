# Visual redlines and useful replacements

This is historical preference context derived from an earlier screenshot and brief, not a current universal contract or validated authorship test. Use it to investigate a rendered weakness, never to ban colours, fonts, libraries or familiar patterns automatically. Current user instructions and accepted brands govern. Identify measurable usability defects separately.

**P** = preference. **U** = potential usability issue requiring observation or measurement. None proves that an interface was made with AI.

| # | Signature | Kind | Default correction | Useful exception or distinction |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Purple-to-blue gradient | P | Choose semantic surfaces and a fitted accent. | Retain an explicitly authorised gradient brand, game world or colour visualisation. |
| 2 | Gradient hero text | P, U | Use solid text and hierarchy through size, weight and placement. | A purposeful display treatment still needs contrast checks across its backgrounds. |
| 3 | Emojis in headings | P | Write descriptive headings; use real subject imagery when useful. | Emoji may be content in messaging or playful products, not a replacement for a label. |
| 4 | Inter everywhere | P | Choose type by role, readability and brand; give existing type a considered hierarchy. | Keep Inter when the current brief or established brand calls for it. Changing fonts alone is not an improvement. |
| 5 | Coloured border cards | P | Group by whitespace, alignment, separators or neutral surfaces. | Coloured edges can identify meaningful state, with a second cue besides colour. |
| 6 | Glassmorphism cards | P, U | Use stable surfaces and intentional elevation. | Native translucency or approved media overlays may fit; verify varying backdrops and fallback. |
| 7 | Low-contrast dark mode | U | Measure actual colour pairs and states; correct failing roles. | Dark mode is legitimate. Decorative and inactive elements have different WCAG applicability. |
| 8 | Three icon boxes in a row | P | Choose the comparison, sequence, list, table or working surface the task needs. | Three genuine comparable options can merit three columns; avoid feature filler. |
| 9 | Badge above headline | P | Begin with topic, task or evidence; remove redundant ornament. | A real deadline, beta status or access restriction may need prominence. |
| 10 | Lucide icons everywhere | P, U | Keep useful familiar symbols; remove redundant decoration and add labels. | Retain a coherent set. Do not substitute novel ambiguous drawings for familiar controls. |
| 11 | Untouched shadcn UI | P | Adapt tokens, composition, density, copy and states while preserving behaviour. | Reliable primitives are useful. A default component does not prove poor usability. |
| 12 | Fade-in on scroll | P, U | Make content available immediately; reserve motion for meaningful transitions. | Necessary disclosures or state transitions can animate with reduced-motion support. |
| 13 | Cursor-following beam | P, U | Remove ambient effects competing with the task or performance. | Cursor effects can belong in an explicitly interactive art or game brief. |

## Broader composition failures

- Avoid a bento grid, sidebar, four KPI tiles or hero/features/testimonials/pricing stack before establishing the job. Use these when the content actually requires them.
- Avoid card nesting, equal prominence for unequal actions, labels behind tooltips, disconnected filters and oversized whitespace around dense work.
- Avoid floating orbs, particles, shimmer, stock sparkle marks, pseudo-3D hover and typewriter effects as automatic signs of “premium”. Give each a purpose or omit it.
- Avoid fake activity feeds, invented testimonials, fabricated revenue, unjustified confidence percentages and controls that merely look functional.
- Avoid flattening creative products into administrative apps. A study planner, restoration viewer, animation canvas and accounting queue need different main surfaces.
- Avoid erasing brand character, shrinking every control, removing all cards or rejecting familiar conventions to correct a generic look.

## Turn a flag into a decision

Record location and role, evidence, preference or standard, consequence and correction. State an exception only when it changes the decision. An import or keyword match begins inspection; it does not finish it.
