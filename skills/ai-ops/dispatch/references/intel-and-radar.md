# Intelligence and frontier radar

For formal due diligence, forecasts or scenarios use oracle. For a full source ledger use deep-research-orchestrator. Use public, professional, verifiable information only.

## Company package

1. Entity fundamentals: legal identity, structure, key personnel, ownership.
2. Financial signals: revenue, funding, profitability, pricing behaviour.
3. Operational: current strategy, hiring patterns, product evolution, partnerships.
4. Reputation and risk: reviews, litigation, regulatory action, adverse media.
5. Competitive position: direct competitors, real versus claimed advantage, trajectory.

## Person package (public professional record)

Professional history (career trajectory, verified credentials, exits, failures); network (key professional relationships, board seats, who vouches publicly, concerning associations); track record (verifiable outcomes, commitments made versus kept); current motivation and pressures as inferred from public statements; red flags (inconsistencies, legal history, gap between claim and verifiable fact). For reading motivation and signals in a relationship, see cipher.

## Market package

Size and structure (total addressable market, major players and share, growth, fragmented or consolidated); competitive landscape (who, what, price, white space); entry barriers (regulatory, capital, relationships, incumbents); customers (who buys, what drives the decision, pain points, what would make them switch). Verdict: attractiveness H/M/L, feasibility H/M/L, timing NOW / LATER / NOT YET, giving ENTER / MONITOR / AVOID.

## Red team

Before finalising: what am I most likely wrong about, what evidence am I ignoring, what would change this entirely, am I seeing what I want to see?

## Frontier radar

Signal changes how he would work, build or trade today. Noise is incremental benchmarks, announcements, hype cycles and pundit commentary.

Watchlist (Shaun's, review for staleness each quarter):
- AI: model capability updates and tooling, multi-agent patterns that work in production, reasoning improvements, persistent memory for agents, AI-native commercial models succeeding at scale. To evaluate when available: mixture of agents, agent-to-agent delegation, self-improving prompt pipelines.
- Markets: RBA posture and AUD, global liquidity cycle, gold and silver structural demand, CFD product and regulatory changes, quantitative strategy crowding. Structural: AI's effect on market microstructure, retail participation waves, macro regime transitions.

Assessment template:

```
DEVELOPMENT: what is it
NOVELTY: genuinely new / incremental / repackaged
MATURITY: experimental / early adoption / crossing mainstream / commoditised
EVIDENCE: strong primary / anecdotal / hype
RELEVANCE: trading H/M/L, AI work H/M/L, commercial H/M/L
TIME SENSITIVITY: act now / watch / aware / background
ACTION: integrate now / experiment within X weeks / monitor / ignore
```
