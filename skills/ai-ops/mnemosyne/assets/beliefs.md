# Beliefs

Working beliefs the plan rests on. One line each. Confidence is a word: near certain, likely, toss-up, unlikely.
When a belief changes, keep the old value in the line with `was:` and the date.

- [2026-01-15] (confidence: likely) (review: 2026-04-15) Statement of the belief. Evidence: what it rests on.
- [2026-02-01] (confidence: toss-up) (review: 2026-03-01) A second belief. Evidence: one source, not yet confirmed. was: likely, changed 2026-02-01 after the supplier's notice.
