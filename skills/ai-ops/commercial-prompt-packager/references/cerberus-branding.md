# Cerberus branding

## Cerberus-branded

- Brand name: Cerberus Market Intelligence.
- Positioning: institutional-grade intelligence and AI tooling for serious operators.
- Tone: direct, authoritative, zero filler; speaks to professionals who do not need hand-holding.
- Quality mark: "Engineered to institutional-grade specifications."
- Designation: "A Cerberus Market Intelligence Asset".
- Where relevant, cite the three-lens framework (macro, technical, sentiment) as a credibility signal.
- Naming convention: `<Function>: A Cerberus Intelligence Asset`.

## White-label or generic

- Strip all Cerberus references, including in examples and file names.
- Neutral professional language.
- The buyer applies their own brand.
