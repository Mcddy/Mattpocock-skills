# Sales copy

## Architecture (produce for every product)

1. **Headline** (8 to 12 words). Lead with the outcome, not the mechanism. Example shape: "Stop wasting hours on X: let AI do it in minutes."
2. **Subheadline** (1 to 2 sentences). Who it is for, what it does, why it is different.
3. **Problem block** (2 to 3 sentences). Name the pain the buyer felt before finding this.
4. **Solution block** (3 to 5 sentences). What it does, how it works at a high level, what changes for the buyer.
5. **What is included** (bullets). Concrete deliverables per tier.
6. **Who this is for** (3 to 5 bullets). "This is for you if..."
7. **Social proof hook.** "Used by <type of professional> to <result>" only if true. Otherwise a visible placeholder: [Add testimonial / result here when available].
8. **Call to action.** Specific: "Get the Pro Kit, $97", not "Buy now".

## Channel adaptations

- **Marketplace listing (Gumroad, Promptbase and similar).** Headline and subheadline first; tight problem and solution (buyers scroll fast); "What is included" prominent; show the higher tier first as a price anchor.
- **Substack offer.** Conversational, written to an existing audience. Open with context ("I've been using this in my own workflow for X months"), frame as early or insider access where true, include a teaser example output.
- **Direct or private client pitch.** No marketing copy. Lead with the problem in their situation, add a one-paragraph capability summary, attach the technical documentation as the credibility signal.
