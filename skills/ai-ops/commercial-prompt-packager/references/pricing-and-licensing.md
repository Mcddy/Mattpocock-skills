# Pricing and licensing

As of the original skill (2025 to early 2026), verify before use. Prices in USD. Compare against current comparable listings before committing.

## Tier template

| Tier | Name | Range | Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entry | Starter / Solo | $9 to $29 | Prompt file and basic usage guide |
| Standard | Pro / Professional | $49 to $149 | Prompt, docs, example outputs, setup guide |
| Premium | Enterprise / Full Kit | $197 to $497 | Everything, plus customisation guide, commercial use licence, optional priority support |
| Bespoke | Custom / Done-for-you | $500 to $2,500+ | Adaptation to the client's specific use case |

Value-based pricing: the question is what it is worth to the buyer, not how long it took to make. Recommend at least three tiers; a single price leaves money on the table.

## Pricing signals

- Sophistication: simple prompt, lower end; full system prompt architecture, higher.
- Specificity: generic utility, lower; niche vertical, higher (less competition, more buyer pain).
- Proof: no track record, lower; demonstrated results justify a premium.
- Audience: consumer or hobbyist, lower; business or professional, higher.
- Exclusivity: unlimited licences, lower per unit; limited seats or exclusive, much higher.

## Licence types

| Licence | Tier | Key terms |
|---|---|---|
| Personal use | Entry | Own projects only. No resale, sharing or client use. |
| Commercial use | Standard | Client work and business use. No resale of the prompt itself. |
| Extended commercial | Premium | Full commercial use including building products on top. No redistribution. |
| White-label / OEM | Bespoke | Buyer may rebrand and resell as their own. Highest price. |
